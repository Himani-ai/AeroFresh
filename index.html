Contributing signals

Component weights

How the risk score works

TRANSPARENT LOGIC
01Temperature deviation

Compares the current reading against an illustrative reference band.

02Humidity deviation

Represents sustained moisture conditions using a simple threshold component.

03Gas-response change

Uses relative variation from a rolling baseline, not a gas concentration.

04Persistence

Adds a small contribution when recent conditions remain outside the reference bands.

The weights and thresholds are illustrative engineering placeholders. Calibrate with real sensor data and validated food-storage studies before drawing operational or food-safety conclusions.
